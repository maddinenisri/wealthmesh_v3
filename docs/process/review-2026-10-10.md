## What the pattern says

1. **Counts improved because checks moved earlier, not because the builder stopped missing things.** The validator
   still finds a real defect almost every slice. The owner now sees fewer faults because the screenshot step and the
   plants catch them first. That is the right shape, but it means the validator is not optional.
2. **The checklist is in danger of being prose again.** Each slice adds one to three lines. At 82 lines, a builder
   cannot hold it, and the validator is the only reader that applies all of it.
3. **The largest remaining risk is the claim, not the code.** Three corrections in this period were about what was
   verified, and one of them (the flake check) misled several reports, including my relays of them. Unexplained failures that appear
   twice and are then "not reproduced" are the same class.
4. **Shared-database e2e traps keep coming back** (pitfalls 41 to 45: a missing key, a short label colliding, a
   region-wide negative, a symbol reused). Each is fixed at the spec, never at the helper.

## Proposals (owner decides; nothing applied)

1. **Prune the build checklist.** Group the 82 lines by theme. Mark a line "retired" if the validator has found nothing
   under it in five slices, and keep a line only with the slice and the defect that added it.
2. **Remove `pattern-reviewer`** from `.claude/agents/`, `AGENTS.md` and `docs/process/workflow.md`, and say so in
   `improvements.md`.
3. **Repeated-run claims carry evidence.** Already in pitfall 53; add to `validator.md` that a claim of N runs without
   the elapsed time counts as one run.
4. **A rule for open failures.** A failure that hits twice and is not reproduced gets a dedicated group in the next
   slice (reproduce with output kept, or close with a reason). Flyway and OwnerCorrection are the first two. After a
   third hit, fix it before new building.
5. **An e2e helper for unique names.** One `uniq(prefix)` that every spec uses for account names, symbols and labels,
   so the shared-database collisions stop at the helper.
6. **Cap the retro row** at about 80 words and the Judgements row at 60, with detail in the slice notes. The retro is
   4,500 words in 43 rows, and nobody reads it.
7. **Review cadence.** Next review after slice 25 (the end of the investments milestone), not every five slices.

## Not changed on purpose

The slice-per-session loop, the two checkpoints, the walkthrough, the Cowork pass, the advisor calls and the plants.
All have a recent retro row showing them useful.

## Outcome (2026-10-10)

All seven applied. Proposal 1 was applied as an upkeep rule and one misplaced item moved, because retiring a line needs the
validator's per-line findings, which are not recorded; the first real pruning happens at the review after slice 25 and
needs the validator to report which checklist lines it found a defect under. Proposal 5 added `uniq()`; no spec uses
it yet, so the next slice that adds an e2e spec uses it and the validator checks that.

| D-028 | 2026-10-04 | Balance correction | A correction is a signed `correction` row: requested Balance minus the Balance on its date, computed by the server at save time (a preview is informational). Never income or spending | Slice 03 checkpoint 1 and validator; a stale preview or a concurrent save must not store a wrong amount, and a retry must not fail because the ledger moved | active |
| D-029 | 2026-10-04 | Fee replaces a correction | An expense dated the same day as a decrease correction, with the same amount, can replace it (kind `expense`, any spending category, the scenario uses Bank fees; spending SQL unchanged). The review offers replacement by default with "Save as a separate expense instead" | Slice 03 checkpoint 1 (owner: follow recommendation); the Balance stays the same and the money counts once as spending | active |
| D-030 | 2026-10-04 | Starting balance and tracking start | The opening stays on the account row (D-017); each correction of its amount or date is a row in `opening_revision` that keeps what it replaced, with reason, who and when. It is never income or spending and adds no entry. The save locks the account row and reads it again; the new date cannot be after any counted entry, and Undo of an entry before the current start is refused (409) | Slice 04; JOURNEY_004, CHECKING_016 | active |
| D-031 | 2026-10-04 | Entry before tracking began | A plain entry dated before the account's start is refused. The UI sends it to a reviewed move of the start, and one call (`POST /accounts/{id}/historical-entries`, kinds expense or income only) saves the start move and the entry in one transaction under the account lock. Every writer of an account's entries (save, replacement, Undo) locks the account row and checks the date on the current row | Slice 04; the validator found entries left before a start that moved at the same time | active |
| D-032 | 2026-10-04 | Supporting statements | A statement is a record (date, balance shown, note), not a file; it never feeds Balance, income or spending. Remove and Undo come with slice 19 | Slice 04, Q-028; SUPPORTING_RECORD_003 | active |
